KibbleKarma

Your pet's happiest, healthiest self — tracked with love.

Feed good. Feel good. Repeat.

KibbleKarma is a self-hosted pet wellness tracker — a cozy, non-clinical companion for logging meals, weight, and health events for the furry members of your family. This is the brand guide: the voice, color, type, and components that make it feel like a hug from a golden retriever.

Nourish Thrive Private & Yours

Brand essence

A hug from a golden retriever

It sits in the sweet spot between a pet-care journal and a wellness dashboard. Not a vet app. Not a cold spreadsheet. It's the digital equivalent of a pet scrapbook that happens to do smart calorie math. The tone is warm, approachable, and a little goofy.

Nourish

Know what goes in the bowl.

Meals, foods, barcode scan, calorie math. Logging a meal should feel like a small act of love, never a chore.

Thrive

Watch them grow (in all the right ways).

Weight trends, health events, gentle charts. Progress is celebrated, never scolded — wellbeing, not diagnosis.

Private & Yours

Your furball's data. Your home server.

Self-hosted, no subscription, no surveillance. No one else's business but yours and your crew's.

The mark

Logo & wordmark

Paw-print yin-yang: the karma cycle of give & get, in brand colors.. The mark, never an emoji, is the favicon. Give it room to breathe; the cozy shadow does the lifting, never a hard outline.

Primary mark — on oat. Clear space ≥ the bowl's height on all sides.

On charcoal — lighten the ramps one step; keep the warm tint.

Do
  • Keep the terracotta/sage yin-yang balance intact.
  • Place on oat, white, sage-50, or terracotta-50 grounds.
  • Pair the mark with the Fredoka wordmark for lockups.
Don't
  • Don't recolor with cool greys, neon, or pure red.
  • Don't add a hard outline or square the bowl off.
  • Don't swap the mark for a 🐾 emoji as a favicon.

Palette

Warm, earthy, cozy

Terracotta warmth, sage calm, butter cheer, oat-cream ground, and a warm charcoal ink. Everything skews warm — peachy, sunlit, golden. No cool greys, no high-contrast B&W, no neon. The ● marks the brand anchor in each ramp.

Typography

Nunito does the work, Fredoka brings the joy

Nunito is the workhorse for headings and body — rounded, friendly, very legible, and it loves to go heavy (ExtraBold 800 is the house weight for titles). Fredoka, chunky and rounded, is reserved for brand and hero moments. Sentence case everywhere.

Iconography

Lucide line icons, warm-tinted

Our icon language is Lucide — sleek, rounded, ~2px stroke with rounded caps that echo Nunito and Fredoka. Emoji are deprecated as functional icons; they may pepper celebratory microcopy sparingly, but warm words carry the tone. One icon per label, leading the text, 18–24px.

Voice & tone

Warm, encouraging, playful, a little silly

The voice is the most distinctive thing about KibbleKarma. It speaks in the second person — possessive and affectionate — and treats the pet as a beloved family member. Never clinical, never anxiety-inducing. The app calls itself "we." Sentence case everywhere, never ALL CAPS.

Components

The building blocks

Reusable primitives, all backed by tokens. Pill buttons, cozy-shadowed cards, soft chips and avatars, gentle inputs, underline tabs, and feedback that celebrates. Press gives a tiny settle; focus shows a soft 3px terracotta ring.

Buttons

Always pills. Primary = terracotta fill; Secondary = sand → warms to terracotta-100 on hover; Ghost = transparent terracotta. Optional leading icon.

Chips & avatars

Five soft tones for chips. Avatars are round tiles holding a Lucide species glyph on a tinted ground.

Forms

Label + rounded input with a sand border that turns terracotta on focus, plus a soft ring. Selects get a soft chevron.
